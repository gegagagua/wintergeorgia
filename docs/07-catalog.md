# 07 — Things to do catalog

Free in season 1. It builds traffic and, more importantly, assembles the partner network that
season 2 monetises.

## Categories

Paragliding, quad bikes, snowmobiles, tubing, horse riding, heliski, bars, restaurants,
après-ski, spa and sulfur baths, ski schools, rental shops.

## Place fields

Name (3 locales), category, resort, photos, price from, opening hours, phone, booking link,
map pin, short editorial description, tags (family friendly, English spoken, card accepted).

## Partner cabinet

A venue owner claims their listing and can update price, hours and events themselves.
Moderation on first claim. From season 2: featured placement (monthly fee) and ticket
commission.

## Rules

- No listing without a photo and a phone number.
- Prices carry an "as of" date; a price older than one season is hidden, not shown stale.
- Rental shops and ski schools are also commission partners — their listings carry a booking
  link that is tracked as a lead.
