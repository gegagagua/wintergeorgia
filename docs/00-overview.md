# 00 — Overview

## Product in one sentence

georgiawinter is the booking and information layer for Georgia's ski season: it sells
transfers and local shuttles, and earns its traffic by being the fastest place to check
road status, snow depth, prices, events and things to do.

## Why it can win

- Booking.com and Airbnb own beds. Nobody owns the *day*: getting there, gear, lessons, events.
- Those services are sold today through Facebook groups, WhatsApp and hotel receptions.
- Transfer commission is 15–25%; hotel commission would be 8–12% against an impossible incumbent.

## Audience and language priority

| Segment | Language | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| International tourist | English (primary) | how to get there, what it costs, is the road safe |
| Regional tourist | Russian | transfers, prices, events |
| Local | Georgian | weather, slopes, events, things to do |
| Hotels / apartment hosts | Georgian, English | sell services to their guests for commission |

English is the primary locale: the domain is English and the search volume is in English.

## Resorts covered

Gudauri, Bakuriani, Tetnuldi, Hatsvali, Goderdzi, Kazbegi (freeride / ski-touring, not a
lift resort — describe it honestly).

## Revenue

| Source | Model | When |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer commission | 15–25% of booking | season 1 |
| Equipment rental | commission with partner shops | season 1 |
| Car rental | affiliate / lead fee | season 1 |
| Accommodation | Booking + Airbnb affiliate links only | season 1 |
| Featured listing in catalog | monthly fee | season 2 |
| Events | promotion + ticket commission | season 2 |
| Site advertising | banners, sponsorship | season 2 |

## Hard constraint: seasonality

Revenue arrives December–April. SEO pages must be indexed before December, which means
content work starts in June. Launching in November misses the first season.

## Out of scope for v1

Hotel inventory and hotel booking. Own vehicle fleet. Native mobile apps. Dynamic pricing.
Ticket sales (events link out in season 1).
